Programme facilitation / Oxford Venture Builder

Testing the process behind a new venture

XiaoYu Limited was engaged for a bounded facilitator assignment within Oxford Venture Builder, helping participants design a business process and use it to test assumptions.

Saïd Business School, University of OxfordVenture development
THE DECISION FRAME01 / STRATEGIC SHIFT
The strategic shiftHow can a founder test the venture's operating assumptions early?
Assumption→Testable process
Map the process, identify assumptions, design an initial iteration and test it.

The assignment gave founders a way to turn an idea about how the venture works into a process they could examine and revise.

The programme need

Early ventures often have a clear idea of the product and a less explicit view of the process needed to deliver, sell and learn from it. For Oxford Venture Builder participants, the useful question was how to design that process before assuming the model would work in practice.

Saïd Business School commissioned a specific facilitator contribution to address that question within the wider programme.

The XiaoYu assignment

The 28 March 2024 engagement letter to XiaoYu Limited specifies an Oxford Venture Builder facilitator role. Its two named deliverables were to help participants understand how to design their business process and use it to validate assumptions, and to demonstrate initial iterations of that process design.

The facilitator service took place in May 2024 as one focused contribution within the wider Oxford Venture Builder programme.

What the case shows

A venture-building programme can benefit from facilitation that links strategic intent to operational experiment. By drawing the process, founders can expose what must happen, which assumptions matter most, and what to test next.

This assignment helped participants make their working assumptions explicit and explore early iterations of how the business could operate. Its value lies in making the venture process visible enough to test and revise.

Why this matters for programme commissioners

For founders exploring a new country, a process map can expose which customer, partner and delivery assumptions need testing locally. XiaoYu’s bounded Oxford Venture Builder assignment helped participants draw and iterate that process; the record does not establish a subsequent international launch.
