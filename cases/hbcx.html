Service-model transformation and wider-market potential / Hebei Chengxin Group

Building a service model for growth across markets

HBCX reframed its offer from chemical inputs to a clean-gold service proposition. A customer trial tested a model that could be assessed with mining customers in other markets.

Hebei Chengxin Group Co., Ltd. (HBCX)Industrial chemicals
THE DECISION FRAME01 / STRATEGIC SHIFT
The strategic shiftWhat could HBCX take to a new market besides a chemical?
Chemical supply→Clean-gold service
The service model joins technical advice, specification and delivery around the customer's goal.

The growth decision was to make clean gold, not chemical volume, the value proposition and build a service model around it.

The decision facing HBCX

HBCX supplied chemicals into a long chain of intermediaries serving the mining industry. As buyers could compare chemical inputs and press on price, remaining a product supplier exposed the business to margin pressure. The underlying customer goal, however, was to process gold effectively. Chemical supply was only one part of that job.

The question became whether HBCX could move closer to that end result using knowledge and infrastructure it already had: technical expertise, laboratories, logistics and access to partners.

The business model work

Value-chain analysis reframed the offer from selling a chemical input to supporting a clean-gold outcome. On-site consulting, chemical-mix specification, workflow scheduling and delivery coordination came together as a service. The wider model considered specialist equipment partners and acquisitions to complete the customer journey.

A customer trial tested the service approach. The team also explored how an outcome-linked price could work, making the commercial model a further question for the business to test.

What the case shows

The shift changed where HBCX could create value. Existing technical capabilities became part of a broader customer solution, with the relationship extending beyond a chemical shipment. The service trial gave the company a way to test that proposition with a customer before treating it as a standard offer.

The strategic lesson is that a service proposition built around the customer's result offers a basis for testing demand in other markets. The record establishes a service trial, not a completed international rollout.

Why this matters for leaders

For leaders seeking international growth, the first question is whether the offer will matter to customers in the next market. HBCX looked beyond the chemical shipment to the mining outcome, then combined technical advice, laboratories, logistics and partners into a service proposition. Testing that model with a customer came before assuming it could scale.
